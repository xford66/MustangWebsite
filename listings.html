Finding Your Perfect Mach 1

The 2023 Mustang Mach 1 is a limited-production model with only a few thousand units produced. Use the search links above to find current listings on major automotive marketplaces.

What to look for:

  • Handling Package (MagneRide suspension, upgraded aero)

  • 6-speed manual transmission (more desirable than 10-speed auto)

Pricing Guide

2023 Mustang Mach 1 values vary based on mileage, condition, and options:

  • Premium Trim: $58,000 - $62,000
  • Handling Package: +$3,000 - $5,000
  • Low Mileage (under 3k): +$2,000 - $4,000
  • Manual Transmission: +$1,000 - $2,000 premium over automatic
